Twelve regulars and — unusually — **three** secrets, tiered: two at 1/144 and one "super secret" at 1/288.

**Do not confuse this with the plush.** There is a separate *Crying Again Vinyl Face Plush* line sharing the name, with a different and smaller roster. Two figures, Baby Blonde and Baby Brown, are ordinary regulars here and **secrets** over there. A collector who checks the wrong list will think they pulled a chase, or fail to realise they did.

*Naming* — the Chinese name is a **renaming, not a translation**: 眼泪工厂系列, "Tear Factory". Anyone translating "Crying Again" into Chinese lands on a name that no Chinese collector uses. The mapping is provable because Pop Mart serves both titles under the same product ID.
