Twelve figures, each an emotional register. A full case of twelve contains no repeats, which makes this set unusually tractable to complete by the case rather than by the box.

*Structure* — the roster maps cleanly onto Plutchik's wheel of emotions, eight primaries and their intensities. That is a useful integrity check: a roster claiming an emotion outside the wheel is probably wrong.
