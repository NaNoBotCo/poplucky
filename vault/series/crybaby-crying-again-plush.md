The plush cousin of *Crying Again*, and the sharpest argument in this catalogue for keeping **form** as its own axis.

Same IP, same series name, six regulars instead of twelve — and the two secrets here, Baby Blonde and Baby Brown, are plain regulars in the figures line. Rarity is a property of a figure *within a form*, never of a name.
