Created by a **Thai** artist working as Molly Yllom — not to be confused with Pop Mart's separate MOLLY character. Crying is the whole premise: the figures weep and are cheerful about it.

*Regional note* — CRYBABY's Thai origin makes it the natural doorway for Thai-language readers, and Thai-market releases around this IP deserve close tracking.

*Naming* — 哭娃 is what Chinese media and Taiwanese retailers call this character, introduced with "also known as" rather than as a product name. Pop Mart keeps CRYBABY in Latin on its own Chinese pages. Recorded as a nickname, which is what it is.
